Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2024 · Effective immediately for all EzyHR users.

⚖️ Demonstration Notice: This privacy policy is an illustrative draft created for the EzyHR software demonstration project. For commercial deployment, policies must be reviewed by legal counsel.

1. Introduction & Scope

EzyHR ("we", "our", or "us") values the trust placed in our Human Resource Management and Payroll software platform. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of personal and organizational data we collect, how it is secured, and the rights you maintain over your workforce records.

2. Information We Collect

In operating the EzyHR platform on behalf of subscribing employers, we process the following categories of information:

  • Employee Profile Information: Full legal names, work and personal email addresses, phone numbers, home mailing addresses, dates of birth, emergency contacts, job titles, and department assignments.
  • Payroll & Financial Data: Bank account numbers, salary structures, tax identification numbers, statutory provident fund numbers, and reimbursement claims.
  • Time & Attendance Records: Web clock-in timestamps, biometric device sync records, leave request histories, and shift scheduling logs.
  • Recruitment Data: Resumes, interview scorecards, feedback notes, and employment contract drafts.

3. How We Use Collected Information

We process information exclusively to deliver core HRMS functionality, specifically to:

  • Calculate automated monthly payroll, gross earnings, and tax withholdings.
  • Process employee leave requests and track annual quota balances.
  • Facilitate digital onboarding checklists and e-signature collections.
  • Generate aggregate workforce analytics and attendance reports for company administrators.
  • Ensure security through tamper-evident administrative audit logs.

4. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard technical safeguards including 256-bit AES encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, strict Role-Based Access Controls (RBAC), and regular automated backup redundancy. Sensitive salary fields are masked and restricted to authorized personnel.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

EzyHR never sells employee personal data or customer directories to advertisers. Data is shared strictly with authorized infrastructure subprocessors (such as cloud hosting providers and transactional email gateways) essential to providing the service under strict non-disclosure obligations.

6. User Rights & Account Deletion

Under applicable privacy frameworks, employees and organizations maintain the right to inspect their records, request rectification of erroneous data, or request permanent deletion of their accounts. For details, please review our Account Deletion Policy.

7. Contact the Privacy Team

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or data processing practices, please reach out to our privacy compliance officer at privacy@ezyhr.example.
