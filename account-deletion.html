Important Considerations Before Deleting

Account deletion is permanent. Once completed, employee profiles, attendance logs, and past tax slips cannot be recovered. In accordance with statutory tax laws, historical payroll registers may be retained for statutory audit periods (typically 7–8 years) before automated cryptographic erasure.
