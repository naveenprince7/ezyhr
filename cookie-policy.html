What are cookies?

Cookies are small text fragments stored on your device by your web browser when visiting websites. They allow websites to remember information about your visit, making subsequent visits easier and the website more functional.

How to clear cookies in your browser

You can also clear or block cookies directly through your browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). Please note that blocking essential cookies may prevent you from authenticating into the EzyHR dashboard.
