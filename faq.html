General Platform

EzyHR is an all-in-one Human Resources Management System (HRMS) built for small and medium-sized businesses, startups, and growing enterprises. It combines employee management, automated payroll, attendance tracking, leave workflows, ATS recruitment, and analytics into one unified modern interface.
